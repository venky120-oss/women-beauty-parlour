Our Story

Where Every Woman Feels at Home

Kishore Beauty Parlour began with a small dream — to create a beauty space in Chennai where women could walk in, breathe out and simply be looked after. Not rushed, not judged, just genuinely cared for by people who love what they do.

Over the years, that dream has grown into a trusted neighbourhood parlour for brides, college students, working professionals, homemakers and grandmothers alike. Many of our clients first came for a simple threading appointment and now trust us with their wedding-day look.

What has never changed is how we work. Every service begins with listening — to your skin, your hair, your schedule and your idea of beautiful. We combine skilled hands with quality, skin-friendly products and spotless hygiene, so you can relax completely.

Today, our team continues to learn the latest techniques in makeup, skin and hair care, while holding on to the warmth and personal attention that made Kishore Beauty Parlour what it is.

Our Mission
