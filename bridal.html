FAQs

Bridal Makeup Questions

How early should I book my bridal makeup?

We recommend booking 2–3 months before your wedding, and even earlier for popular muhurtham dates during the wedding season.

Is a makeup trial included?

Trials are included in our Premium and Elite packages. For the Basic package, a trial can be added for a small fee that is adjusted if you book.

Do you come to the wedding venue or home?

Yes, we provide on-location bridal makeup across Chennai. It is included in the Elite package and available as an add-on for others. Travel charges may apply outside city limits.

How long does bridal makeup take?

Plan for about 2.5 to 4 hours for complete bridal makeup, hairstyle, draping and jewellery setting.

Will my makeup last through a long, hot day?

Yes. We use long-wear, sweat-resistant products and setting techniques suited to Chennai's climate, plus touch-ups in Premium and Elite packages.

What is the difference between HD and airbrush makeup?

HD makeup uses light-reflecting products for a natural, camera-friendly finish. Airbrush makeup is sprayed on for an ultra-smooth, lightweight and long-lasting finish.

Do you do makeup for the bride's family and bridesmaids?

Yes! We offer party makeup, hairstyling and draping for family and friends, with special group pricing.

Which products do you use?

We use trusted professional brands that are suitable for Indian skin tones. Let us know about any allergies or sensitivities during consultation.

How do I confirm my booking?

Share your date and venue on WhatsApp. Once availability is confirmed, a small advance secures your date.

What should I do to prepare my skin?

Start a pre-bridal plan 4–8 weeks ahead, stay hydrated, sleep well and avoid trying new skincare products in the last two weeks.
