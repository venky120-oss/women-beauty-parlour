Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kishore Beauty Parlour ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, book an appointment or contact us, and how we use and protect it. We follow the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in, preferred date and time, and any notes you add in our booking or contact forms.
  • Messages: information you share with us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.
  • Service information: details relevant to your treatment, such as skin type, allergies or hair concerns, which you choose to share with us so we can serve you safely.
  • Technical information: basic data your browser sends automatically, such as device type and pages visited, if analytics tools are used.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To book, confirm, reschedule or remind you about appointments.
  • To answer your questions and provide the services you request.
  • To personalise treatments and keep you safe (for example, noting allergies).
  • To send you offers or updates — only if you have asked to receive them. You can opt out at any time.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website uses a few trusted third-party services to work properly:

  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you tap a WhatsApp button, your message is sent through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps: our embedded map is provided by Google and may collect usage data according to Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts and image/icon providers: used to display fonts, photos and icons; these services may receive your IP address when loading content.
  • Social media links: Instagram, Facebook and YouTube have their own privacy policies when you visit them.

4. Cookies

Our website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services listed above (such as Google Maps) may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; the website will continue to work.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. We keep your details only for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain appointment records, or meet legal requirements, after which they are deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update your information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, unless we must keep it for legal reasons.
  • Withdraw your consent to receive offers or messages at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services for clients under 18 are provided with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children online without such consent.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date.
